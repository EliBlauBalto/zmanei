    How these times are worked out

    The plane is projected along the great-circle path from JFK to FCO (4,266 mi), climbing 25 min and descending 30 min. Once airborne, live ADS-B or phone GPS re-anchors the route. Each zman is solved for the exact spot you’ll be over at that moment.

    Netz is shown both from the plane (at 37,000 ft the horizon dips ~3.4°, so sunrise is ~13 min earlier) and on the ground below. Halachic hours use sea-level sunrise and sunset under the plane. If delayed, tap Edit times above to recalculate everything. Opinions differ on in-flight zmanim; ask your rav.
