    How these times are calculated

    The plane is projected along the great-circle track from JFK to FCO at cruising altitude (37,000 ft), climbing 25 minutes and descending 30 minutes. Once airborne, live ADS-B or phone GPS re-anchors the path. Each zman is solved for the exact coordinates the aircraft reaches at that moment. Netz is given twice: from the cabin (horizon dip ~3.4° makes sunrise ~13 minutes earlier) and on the ground below. Halachic hours use sea-level sunrise and sunset beneath the plane. Opinions differ on in-flight zmanim; consult your rav.
